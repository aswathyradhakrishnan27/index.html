03 · CUSTOMER ASSIST

Customer Assist

Built Customer Assist as a single enterprise AI project with two connected workstreams: a real-time Voice Bot and an Intelligent FAQ Chatbot.

TASK 01 · CUSTOMER ASSIST VOICE BOT

Developed real-time voice and text interaction, initially supporting FAQs for three brands and later migrating to the OneAI platform. Implemented multi-agent routing, Novasonic speech-to-text models, and a PII/PHI masking pipeline using LangFuse traces before converting masked responses back to audio.

TASK 02 · CUSTOMER ASSIST INTELLIGENT FAQ CHATBOT

Designed an FAQ-focused multi-agent assistant with a Master Orchestrator coordinating Chitchat, RAG, and Escalation agents, along with domain agents for Admin, Troubleshooting, and Billing. Live tracing with LangFuse provides visibility into agent execution and conversational flow.
